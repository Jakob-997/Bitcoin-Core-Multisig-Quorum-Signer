AFTER SETUP

Back up descriptor-signer-wallets/ on secure persistent/offline storage before
shutting down Tails. Private wallet backups are sensitive.

After create: give the public [origin]xpub to your coordinator.
After import: independently check the policy, cosigners and receive/change
addresses. Test signing and backup restoration with disposable funds.

If import is interrupted, rerun sh tails.sh import with the same wallet and
descriptor. Do not fund an incomplete policy.
