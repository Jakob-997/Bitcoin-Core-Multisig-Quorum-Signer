POST-CREATION GUIDE

Your Bitcoin Core descriptor signer has been created.

Persistent wallet data is in:

descriptor-signer-wallets/

inside this project folder.

1. BACK UP THE SIGNER

Create and verify an offline backup before funding the wallet policy.

The backup contains private signing material. Anyone who obtains it may be able to sign whenever a spending path involving this signer is available.

2. VERIFY THE PUBLIC POLICY

On the coordinator side, independently verify the exact public descriptor you intended.

Depending on the policy, check:

- signer fingerprints and account origins;
- thresholds;
- receive/change derivation;
- Miniscript branches;
- relative or absolute timelocks;
- Taproot script paths;
- recovery paths;
- the first several receive/change addresses.

Do not treat successful Core import as proof that the policy is correct.

3. TEST WITH DISPOSABLE FUNDS

Create a PSBT that exercises a path involving this signer.

On the offline signer:
- inspect destination;
- inspect amount;
- inspect fee;
- sign with Bitcoin Core.

Complete the policy's required signatures and verify the transaction before broadcast.

For timelocked/recovery policies, test those paths separately on regtest/test infrastructure where practical.

4. VERIFY RESTORE

Restore the signer backup on an offline system.

Confirm:
- the wallet loads;
- the same public descriptor is present;
- expected addresses match;
- the local signer can sign a disposable PSBT for an applicable path.

5. STORE

After testing:
- shut down the offline system;
- remove the Tails USB;
- store backups according to your threat model;
- keep enough independent signer material separated to preserve the wallet policy.

ONLINE COORDINATOR IS UNTRUSTED

The coordinator may be compromised.

Verify transaction details and the intended wallet policy independently before signing.
