POST-CREATION GUIDE

Your Bitcoin Core multisig signer has been created.

The persistent wallet data is in:

signer-wallets/

inside this project folder.

Keep this computer and the signer backup private until the full quorum has been verified.

1. BACK UP THE SIGNER WALLET

Create at least one verified backup of the completed Bitcoin Core wallet before funding the quorum.

The wallet contains private signing material. Anyone who obtains it may be able to produce this signer's share of a multisig signature.

Do not place the private signer wallet on an online computer.

2. VERIFY THE PUBLIC QUORUM

On the coordinator/watch-only side, verify the final public descriptor is the exact descriptor you intended.

Confirm:

- the intended M-of-N threshold;
- the expected signer fingerprints;
- the expected BIP87 or BIP48 native-P2WSH account paths (this Core signer uses BIP87 account 0);
- wsh(sortedmulti(...));
- /<0;1>/* receive/change derivation;
- the same first receive address is derived independently by the signer and the coordinator.

Do not fund an address that has only been verified on an online machine.

3. TEST THE SIGNER WITH DISPOSABLE FUNDS

Before meaningful use:

- send a small disposable amount to an address independently verified by the offline signer;
- create an unsigned PSBT on the online coordinator;
- transfer the PSBT to the offline signer;
- load it in Bitcoin Core;
- independently verify destination, amount, fee, and transaction details;
- sign it;
- combine the required signatures;
- broadcast only after the intended threshold is reached.

If the quorum has more signers than the threshold, test each signer independently from the original unsigned PSBT so every backup is proven capable of signing.

4. VERIFY RESTORE

Copy the signer backup to temporary writable storage on an offline system and confirm Bitcoin Core can load it.

Verify the restored wallet derives the same multisig receive address and can sign a disposable test PSBT.

5. STORE THE SIGNER

After backup and testing are complete:

- shut down the offline computer;
- remove the Tails USB;
- move the signer backup directly to its intended secure storage;
- keep it separate from other quorum signers according to your threat model.

ONLINE COMPUTER IS UNTRUSTED

The online coordinator may be compromised.

Before signing, trust only transaction details independently verified on the offline signer. Never approve a transaction solely because the online computer says it is correct.

Finished.
