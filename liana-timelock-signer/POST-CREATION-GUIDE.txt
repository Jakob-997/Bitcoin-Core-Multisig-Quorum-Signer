POST-CREATION GUIDE

The Bitcoin Core Liana signer has been created.

Persistent wallet data is in:

liana-signer-wallets/

inside this folder.

1. BACK UP THE SIGNER

Create and verify an offline backup of the completed Bitcoin Core wallet before funding the Liana policy.

2. VERIFY THE LIANA POLICY

In Liana, verify:

- every intended signer;
- the primary spending path;
- every recovery spending path;
- every relative timelock;
- the expected derivation origins;
- the same receive address derived independently from the final descriptor.

Do not treat successful import into Core as proof that the Liana policy is the one you intended.

3. TEST THE APPLICABLE SPENDING PATH

Use disposable funds.

Create a PSBT through Liana using a path involving this signer. On the offline signer, inspect the transaction details and sign with Bitcoin Core. Complete the required signatures and verify the transaction before broadcast.

4. TEST RECOVERY LOGIC

On regtest/test infrastructure where practical, exercise the timelocked recovery path as well. A successful primary-path signature does not prove the recovery path is correct.

5. VERIFY RESTORE

Restore the signer backup on an offline system. Confirm:

- the wallet loads;
- the same descriptor is active;
- the same addresses are derived;
- the signer can sign a disposable PSBT for an applicable path.

6. STORE

After testing:

- shut down the offline system;
- store the signer backup according to your threat model;
- keep it separate from enough other signer material to satisfy the policy.

Taproot Liana descriptors are outside this version's reviewed scope.
